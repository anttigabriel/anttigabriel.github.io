# Oak Forest Walk

October 11, 2026

I took a walk today. I visited a small protected oak forest. I love being in a deciduous forest. It reminds me of Switzerland, which I visited couple of years ago.
The oaks I saw today were thick and looked old. A bit of bird chirping could be heard. I took a stroll inside the forest and found a table. Maybe I could have a morning coffee here some day.

Having stood there for a while, I turned back and continued my journey at a countryside I was surrounded by. Fields all around me. And not a single person. Peaceful it was, indeed.
