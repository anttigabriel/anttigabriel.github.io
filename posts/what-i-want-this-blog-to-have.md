# What I want this blog to have

October 12, 2026

I took a walk today. My plan was to go to a bridge to see the river current streaming strongly and loudly. On my way to the bridge, I saw an interesting spot, also by the river, and went there.
I stood by the river on a big rock covered with moss. A white plastic garden chair was lying upside down on the rock. A boat full of autumn leaves was at the shore, it had not been used in a while, probably.
On the way back I started to think about what I want my blog to have.

1. Night mode, where instead of a sun there is a moon in the background.
2. Categorisation of posts.
3. Ability to search for older posts.
4. Ability to browse all my posts in a categorised view.

Here are some of the things that I ended up thinking of. I am not sure how I will do either of those things, but perhaps I'll figure it out.
